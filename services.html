Air conditioning

Car air-con is one of the biggest parts of the workshop. Every system is checked properly first — pressures, leaks, compressor operation and electrical control — so the fix is the right fix.

  • Leak finding & repair
  • High-pressure & low-pressure hoses
  • Compressor faults & noise
  • Evaporator replacement
  • HVAC module & control faults

Diagnostics & fault finding

Warning lights, no-starts, intermittent faults and ‘electrical gremlins’. Using the latest G-Scan and Topdon scan tools, faults are traced back to the actual cause.

  • Engine & dash warning lights
  • Airbag (SRS) light faults
  • No-start & random no-start
  • Intermittent electrical faults
  • Module programming
  • Bad connections, terminals & earths
