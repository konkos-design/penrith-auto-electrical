The story

One man. One bench. Done right.

Tucked away at Units 1&2, 15 Peachtree Road in Penrith, PAE has been looking after local drivers for years — some of our Google reviews go back almost a decade.

John runs the tools; Mum runs the front counter. That's the whole team, and it's exactly why customers keep coming back: the person diagnosing your car is the person you talk to. No service advisors, no upsells, no guessing.

Plenty of jobs make it onto Instagram and Facebook too — over 11,000 posts showing real faults, real fixes and the occasional head-scratcher.
