The A/C process

Inspect. Repair. Regas.

01

Inspect

System pressures, compressor operation and the electrical controls are checked.

02

Find the leak

If gas is low, the leak is tracked down — hoses, fittings, condenser or evaporator.

03

Repair

Faulty parts are repaired or replaced and the system is sealed up properly.

04

Vacuum & regas

The system is pulled down on vacuum, regassed and tested for cold air.

Started somewhere else? If another workshop has already started work on your air-con, please have them complete that job first. We're happy to help with A/C systems from the start.
