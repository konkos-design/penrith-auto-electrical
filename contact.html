FAQ

Good to know

Quick answers before you call.

Call 02 4722 9750
Do I need to book?
Yes — bookings and appointments are required. Give John a call on 02 4722 9750, describe the problem and a time will be organised to get your vehicle in.
Are you open on weekends?
No. The workshop is open Monday to Thursday 8am–4:30pm and Friday 8am–1pm. Saturday and Sunday are closed.
Do you work on German cars?
No, the workshop doesn't work on German vehicles. If you're not sure whether we can help with your make or model, please call first.
Another workshop started on my air-con. Can you finish it?
If another workshop has already started work on your A/C system, please have them complete that job. We're happy to look after A/C systems from the start.
What vehicles do you work on?
Everyday cars, utes, 4WDs, vans, minibuses and tow trucks — plus trailers, RTVs and small machinery with 12V systems.
Where are you located?
Units 1&2, 15 Peachtree Road, Penrith NSW 2750 — in the industrial area off Castlereagh Road.
